## What's new

**Voice mode.** Studywise can read your course aloud with ElevenLabs while you go through a week. Each notes card is read out, and so is an explanation once you press **Explain**. Questions and flashcards are never read out. Turn it on in **Settings → Voice**:

- Paste your ElevenLabs API key. It's encrypted on your computer and only ever sent to ElevenLabs.
- Pick a voice from your ElevenLabs voices, or paste any voice ID.
- Choose the model. **Eleven v4 Turbo** is the default (fast, with audio tags); Eleven v4, v3 and Flash v2.5 are there too.
- **Auto-tag**: Claude adds audio tags like [curious], [short pause] or [reassuring] so it sounds like someone teaching, without changing a word. Write a **voice prompt** to say how it should sound.
- **Play automatically** as each card opens, or **when I click** its Read aloud button.

Code, formulas and diagrams are mentioned rather than read out symbol by symbol. Audio is kept on your computer, so a card you've heard before plays straight away, even offline, and doesn't use your ElevenLabs characters again.

**This week on the Courses page.** The Courses page works out which week you're on: the highest week number in any of your courses (week 9 once a course has a week 9). Every course whose week 9 you haven't finished shows how far through it you are, say "Week 9 · 2 of 5 topics done", and clicking it opens that week. A line above your courses says how many are still to review. Once you've done every topic in a course's week, it stops showing. Reading weeks and weeks without topics yet are left out.

**Smaller things**
- The Android app builds again (merging copies of a course had broken it).

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

Otherwise: Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Android, download `Studywise-android.apk`. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
