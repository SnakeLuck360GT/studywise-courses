## What's new

**Notes are in revision order.** In the Notes section, notes in each folder are now listed in the order you'd revise them, instead of newest first.

- A course's notes go by week, then by topic, in the same order as the course.
- Other notes go by the week they're for (the note's Week, or "Week 3" in its title), then by lecture number ("Lecture 5"). Notes with no week go at the end.
- New notes are put in the right place as soon as they're made, whether from writing a course topic or generating notes. A note you move to another folder takes its place there.

**Put your existing notes in order.** Notes you made before this version stay where they were until you ask. Click **Revision order** above the notes list to sort the folder you have open, and the folders inside it. With **All notes** selected, it sorts every folder.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

Otherwise, on Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
