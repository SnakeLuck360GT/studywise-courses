## What's new

**Ask for weeks, and donate your AI usage to make them.** On the Marketplace there's a new **Requests** tab:

- **Ask for a week** of any course that isn't on the marketplace yet. Modules have 12 weeks, with week 6 as reading week. If someone already asked for it, your ask counts as a vote.
- **Vote** for the weeks you want. When one is made, it's added to your courses by itself the next time you sync with the cloud.
- **Have the slides?** Add them to a request, so someone can make it.
- **Ask for help with one of your own weeks.** If it's planned but not written, your plan goes with it, so nobody has to plan it again. When it's done, it comes back into your week with your progress kept.
- **Published weeks with topics nobody wrote** have an **Ask for the rest** button. Whoever writes them adds them to that week, for everyone.

**A task a day.** Turn on **Donate my AI usage** and, at most once a day, you're given the most wanted week that has slides. It shows as a banner on Home. **Make it** plans and writes the week with your own AI and publishes it for everyone who asked; it goes into your courses too. If you run out of limit partway, **Carry on** picks up where it stopped. You can skip any day, or turn tasks off.

**A marketplace course shows all 12 weeks:** which are there, which people have asked for (click to vote), and which nobody has (click to ask).

**How much of your limit writing will take.** Planned topics, the week page, the Write dialog, tasks and requests show roughly how much of your Claude 5-hour limit writing will use. It learns from your own writing as you go; until it has measured a few topics, it's a rough guess.

## What's fixed

- **Running out of limit while writing.** If a topic didn't get written (at any level of detail, quick, standard or in-depth) and someone has since published that week with it written, opening the course fills it in from theirs. Only what was missing comes in, nothing of yours changes, and the warning goes. A week whose planning failed takes the whole published copy.
- Warnings about writing or planning that failed now go after a day.
- Adding a shared or marketplace week into a week whose planning had failed no longer leaves it marked as failed.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart. On a Mac, the update banner's **Download** button opens it.

Otherwise: Mac, download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Android, download `Studywise-android.apk`. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
