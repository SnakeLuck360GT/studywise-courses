## What's new

**Quick review is much quicker.** A quick review now sticks to the main ideas, the things most likely to come up in the exam, in 2–4 short sections of bullets and tables. Minor details, extra examples and side points are left out, so a topic takes about a third of the time of the full notes. Topics you already have a quick review for can be written again to get the new, shorter version.

**Tell the AI what kind of course it is.** When you make a course, tick any of **Theory heavy**, **Practical / coding heavy** and **Formula & method heavy** (or change it later from the course page). Everything the AI writes for the course is tailored to it:

- Theory heavy: the focus is on understanding why, how ideas link and exact definitions, with more explain and scenario questions.
- Practical / coding heavy: every idea is taught through a small worked example explained line by line, with questions on reading, predicting and fixing code.
- Formula & method heavy: every formula is explained symbol by symbol with a worked example, methods come as numbered steps, and questions have you apply them.

**End-of-week tests.** When you plan a week, tick **Also write an end-of-week test** and pick **Easy**, **Medium** or **Hard**. You get a multiple-choice test across every topic of the week. Take it from the week page once you've studied the week: every answer is marked with why, and at the end you see your score, how you did on each topic and the ones you missed. Weeks you've already planned can get one from the week page too. Your best and last scores are kept.

**Cut flashcards down to key content.** On a week, choose **Cut down flashcards…** and pick how far: **A little**, **Key content** (about half) or **Essentials only** (about a quarter). The AI suggests which cards to cut, and you see the list and untick any you want to keep before anything is removed.

**More questions while you learn.** Settings → General → Courses → **Questions while learning a topic**: Usual, More or Lots. More and Lots give you more questions after each section and in the topic test, for topics written from now on.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

Otherwise, on Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
