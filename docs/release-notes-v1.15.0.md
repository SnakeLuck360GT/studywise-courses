## What's new

**Choose which weeks you're reviewing.** Under the cards-due count on Home there's a new **Choose weeks** button. Tick the weeks of each course (and any other decks) you want to review; the count and **Start reviewing** follow what you picked, and it stays picked until you change it. **Everything**, **Priority weeks only** and **Only what's due** set it in one click.

**Priority weeks.** Star a week to make it a priority: its due cards come before the rest, on Home and in a course's review. Home says how many come from priority weeks first. Star weeks in **Choose weeks** or on the Tracker.

**The Tracker.** A new page in the top bar (`g t`) lists every week of every course. For each one, tick **Learnt** and **Revised**, and rate your **confidence** from 1 to 5 (click the same level again to clear it). Totals for each course and overall sit at the top, and you can show only weeks still to do or only priority weeks. Click a week to open it.

Your ticks, ratings and priority weeks sync to your other devices. They're yours, so they don't go into shared or published weeks.

## Download

This release is Windows only for now. The Mac and Android downloads will be added to it soon; until then, they stay on 1.14.2.

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart. On a Mac, the update banner's **Download** button opens it.

Otherwise: Mac, download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Android, download `Studywise-android.apk`. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
