## What's new

**Update a marketplace week in place.** When a week you added from the marketplace has been published again, **Update** next to **Updated since you added it** brings your copy up to date. A course's page and **Published by you** have **Update N weeks** to do them all. You get the new notes, questions, flashcards and files, and you carry on from the first section you haven't studied. Your flashcard reviews and the cards you made yourself stay. No AI is used. **Add again** still makes a separate copy.

**Merging keeps every detail.** When two copies of a week cover the same topic, Claude now combines their notes into one set that keeps every fact, example, formula and explanation from both, taking out only what's repeated. Each combined section is checked, and if anything would be lost that part is merged the old way instead. This works for duplicate weeks in your courses, copies of a course and versions on the marketplace. Merging needs a connection now: in offline mode, Merge offers to turn offline mode off first.

**Merge copies of a course.** If two of your devices each got the same course (say, one downloaded it from the marketplace), the Courses page points out the copies with **Merge copies**. Everything goes into one course, keeping your furthest progress and your flashcard history.

**Back and forward.** On Windows, a Forward button sits next to Back in the top bar. The back and forward side buttons work on any mouse, and on a Mac you can swipe with two fingers on the trackpad.

**Smaller things**
- Marketplace is back in the top bar, between Notes and Settings. When the bar is tight, the app's name beside the logo hides before the links fold into the menu.
- The Courses tab always opens the Courses page. A topic you left partway shows there with **Continue**.
- Cancelling an AI job on Windows no longer gets stuck on "Measuring usage…".

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart. On a Mac, the update banner's **Download** button opens it.

Otherwise: Mac, download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Android, download `Studywise-android.apk`. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
