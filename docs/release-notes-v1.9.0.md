## What's new

**Add new notes to a week.** Got more material after a week was planned, like extra lecture notes, a summary or another set of slides? Open the week and choose **Add new notes**. Paste your notes, add files, or both.

- Claude compares them with the week's topics and adds only what's new. Something that belongs in an existing topic becomes new sections at the end of it. A separate idea becomes a new topic.
- What you've already studied stays exactly as it is. A topic you've written gets only the new sections, written to follow on from what's there, in every version you have of it (standard, quick revision and so on).
- **You can tell what's new.** Sections you've reviewed keep their tick. New ones are marked **New** until you've been through them, the topic says how many new sections it has, and the week says how many are left to review. New topics are marked **New topic**.
- If you'd finished the topic, **Continue** takes you straight to the first new section. Your test score and progress are kept.
- New flashcards for the new material go into your recall cards, and a few test questions are added to the topic test.
- Sharing or publishing the week includes the new sections as ordinary sections.

**Explain it like I'm 12.** A new write-up mode, next to Standard, Quick revision and In-depth.

- Everyday words, short sentences, and a simple example from ordinary life for every idea, then how it links back to the real thing.
- One small idea per section, each ending with the whole idea in one line, and quick checks along the way.
- It still covers everything you need for the exam, including the exact wording of definitions you might be tested on, after the simple version.
- Like the other modes, it's saved alongside the notes you already have, and you can switch between them.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

Otherwise, on Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
