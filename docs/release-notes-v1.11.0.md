## What's new

**Sync and back up through the cloud.** Studywise can now keep a copy of your whole library (courses, decks, notes, progress, and the slides and transcripts your courses are made from) on your marketplace server, and sync your computers and phone through it. They don't need to be on the same Wi‑Fi or switched on at the same time, and nothing is lost if a device is. A new computer gets everything by turning it on there.

- Turn it on in **Settings → Devices → Cloud sync & backup** (Studywise asks once if you're connected to a marketplace). It uses your marketplace account.
- You can see how much space you're using (5 GB each), which devices are syncing, and your daily backups (kept for 14 days).
- On your phone, open **Settings → Sync → Sync through the cloud**. Pairing the phone with your computer is still how its AI features (Explain, marking answers) use your Claude login.
- Linking two computers isn't needed any more: turn on cloud sync on both.

**Reading week.** When you add a week 6, you can mark it as reading week: no lectures and nothing to plan, though you can still add readings. A week 6's ⋯ menu can mark it too, and a course that skips from week 5 to week 7 offers to add one. **Add next week** now follows on from your highest week number.

**Report a week on the marketplace.** Someone else's week has a flag button: tell the marketplace's admins what's wrong with it and they'll take a look. Its author isn't told who reported it.

**Announcements.** When your marketplace's admin posts a message (say, maintenance tonight), it shows in a bar under the top bar until you close it.

**Smaller things**
- Match-up questions are joined with lines instead of colours, with more room between the two sides.
- The top bar shows a green wifi icon while you're online. Click it to study offline.
- Marketplace is back to opening from the Courses page, and Courses stays highlighted while you're in it.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart. On a Mac, the update banner's **Download** button opens it.

Otherwise: Mac, download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Android, download `Studywise-android.apk`. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
