## What's new

**Weeks you asked for pop up the moment they're done.** When someone finishes a week you asked or voted for on the Marketplace, a card appears straight away on every computer of yours that's open: **Add it** puts it in your course. Close the card and it's added by itself at the next sync instead.

**Deadlines on Home.** In **Settings → General → Home**, choose **Deadlines and tasks** to see your next deadline instead of time studied:

- Upload your deadlines spreadsheet (.xlsx or .csv). A term calendar with a **D** on each deadline's day works, and so does a simple table of names and dates.
- See how long until the next deadline, and the one after. Tick deadlines off as you hand them in.
- Keep your own list of tasks to tick off.

Your deadlines and tasks sync to your other devices.

**A tidier Marketplace.** Donating your AI usage and the weeks people want have their own **Wanted weeks** tab. The main page keeps a banner under the search that takes you there.

**Courses page.** **Tracker** is now a button on the Courses page instead of in the top bar. To add a week a friend shared with you, use **Add a shared week here…** in that week's ⋯ menu.

**Dismiss failures.** "Planning didn't finish" and topics that didn't write now have a ✕.

**Fewer Settings tabs.** Ten tabs are now six: General, AI, Prompts & actions, Voice, Devices & marketplace, and Jobs.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart. On a Mac, the update banner's **Download** button opens it.

Otherwise: Mac, download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Android, download `Studywise-android.apk`. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
