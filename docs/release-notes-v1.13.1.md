## What's fixed

**Voice mode no longer gets stuck on "Getting voice…".** With a slow AI model, adding audio tags could take minutes before anything was read out, and cards you'd already moved past kept the voice busy. Now:

- Each card starts reading within seconds: its first few sentences are prepared first, and the rest while it plays.
- If the AI takes too long to add audio tags, that part is read without them instead of waiting.
- Moving to the next card stops the last one's audio from being made, so it doesn't hold up the new card.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

Otherwise: Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Android, download `Studywise-android.apk`. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
