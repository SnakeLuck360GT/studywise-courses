## What's new

**Your missing weeks, on the Marketplace's front page.** Open the Marketplace and the first thing you see is the weeks of your courses you don't have yet: any week up to the latest one you've got to that has nothing written (week 6, reading week, never counts).

- They're **asked for automatically**, so when someone makes one, it comes into your course the next time you sync with the cloud. Turn off **Ask for them automatically** if you'd rather ask yourself.
- **Upload slides** for any of them, so someone donating their usage can make the week. If the week already has slides in your course, **Share my files** sends those in one click.
- Weeks that are on the marketplace already are grouped by course, with **Add them** to bring them all in without generating anything.
- **Slides wanted for your courses** lists weeks other people asked for that nobody has added slides to yet. If you have them, upload them.

Below that: today's task and the weeks people want most, then the courses. The separate Requests tab is gone; everything is on the front page.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart. On a Mac, the update banner's **Download** button opens it.

Otherwise: Mac, download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Android, download `Studywise-android.apk`. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
