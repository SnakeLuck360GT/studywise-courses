## What's new

**Flashcards work like StudySmarter.** Cards come in sets of 15, and you mark each one red, yellow or green (keys **1**, **2**, **3**, or **←** and **→**; Space flips):

- The first green turns a card light green and sends it to the back of the pile. Get it right again when it comes back and it's learnt (dark green, with a tick).
- Yellow sends a card to the back; red brings it back three cards later. Either way you go on until it's green twice.
- Learnt cards come back for review after 1 day, then 3, 7, 14, 30 days and so on, further apart each time you get them right. On a review one green is enough; yellow or red means learning it again, and it starts over at 1 day.
- The bars along the top show the pile in order, so you can see where each card has gone.

This is now the standard way flashcards are scheduled, for decks and for course key terms. The old schedulers are still in Settings → General if you prefer them.

**Use the arrow keys everywhere.** Arrow keys move between the buttons and links on any page, and Enter presses the one you're on. In the notes list, → opens a folder and ← closes it.

**Notes are easier to find your way around.** Folders open at the top instead of the bottom, the list remembers which folders you had open, and a note shows which folder it's in, so you can click straight back.

**Merging weeks spots repeated questions.** When two copies of a week are merged, the AI now also checks for questions that ask the same thing in other words, and keeps just the fuller one. It only compares; it never rewrites or shortens a question, and a question that adds anything stays. If the AI isn't available, the merge goes ahead as before.

Also includes everything in 1.10.0.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart. On a Mac, the update banner's **Download** button opens it.

Otherwise: Mac, download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
