## What's new

**Make any wanted week now.** On the Marketplace, a week someone asked for that has slides or a plan shows **Make it**. Anyone can donate their AI to it straight away, a friend on the same course included, instead of waiting to be given it as a daily task. Someone else's plan is used, so it isn't planned again.

**Running out of limit partway is fine.** Weeks are made topic by topic. If you run out partway, the topics you wrote are published, and the week stays wanted with just the topics left, for the next person.

**Planned but not written? Help is asked for by itself.** If you plan a week and haven't started writing it a minute later, it's asked for on the Marketplace with your plan, so someone with usage to spare can write it. It comes back into your week when it's done. Turn off **Ask for them automatically** on the Missing weeks tab to stop this.

**Missing weeks have their own tab** on the Marketplace. The front page shows a short line when you have any.

**Weeks with no content.** Marketplace admins can mark weeks of a course that have no lectures. They show like reading week and are never asked for or listed as missing.

**From 1.15.0** (the first Mac and Android build with these):

- **Choose which weeks you're reviewing.** Under the cards-due count on Home, **Choose weeks** lets you tick the weeks of each course you want to review. **Everything**, **Priority weeks only** and **Only what's due** set it in one click.
- **Priority weeks.** Star a week and its due cards come before the rest.
- **The Tracker.** A new page in the top bar (`g t`) lists every week of every course: tick **Learnt** and **Revised**, and rate your **confidence** from 1 to 5. Your ticks sync to your other devices and stay out of shared and published weeks.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart. On a Mac, the update banner's **Download** button opens it.

Otherwise: Mac, download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Android, download `Studywise-android.apk`. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
